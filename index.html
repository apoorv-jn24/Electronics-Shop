Your Neighbourhood Electrical Expert

Everest Electricals has been serving homes and businesses in Saroorpur Kalan and the wider Baghpat district since 2005. We specialise in quality wiring materials, LED lighting, switches, fans, MCBs, distribution boards, and professional electrical repairs.

Whether you need a single MCB or materials for a complete home wiring project, our experienced team will point you to the right product at the right price — and fix any electrical fault quickly and safely.

Areas we serve: Saroorpur Kalan, Baraut, Baghpat, Chhaprauli, Pilana, Kirthali & surrounding villages in Western UP.
